BGG weight: 1 = light, 3 = medium, 5 = heavy

Prototype v4 — filters modeled on the "Selectinator" tab of Jerry's spreadsheet (player count, type, play time and complexity as min/max ranges, mechanics and theme), plus an optional youngest-player-age check carried over from the original concept. Play time and complexity use each game's average across its listed range. Mechanics/Theme is Claude's split of the spreadsheet's single Tags list using standard BGG terminology — worth a review. Games without manual tagging in the spreadsheet show as uncategorized, and games without a saved BGG image show a placeholder.
